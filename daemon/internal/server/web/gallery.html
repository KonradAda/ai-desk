Gags while working

Zapped by its own spell: wand up, lightning, skeleton, soot, shake it off.

Reading the spellbook: takes it out, reads, turns the page, reads on, puts it away.

Hat on fire: fiery sparks, a fireball off the screen, a spark sets the hat alight, it burns unnoticed, a fright, water puts it out.

Rained on: droplets, a stream of water off the screen, a quiet moment, rain, soaked and cross, dries off.
