Gags while working

Zapped by its own spell: wand up, lightning, skeleton, soot, shake it off.

Reading the spellbook: takes it out, reads, turns the page, reads on, puts it away.

Hat blown off: wand, breeze, a gust takes the hat, runs for it, puts it on, runs back.

Hat on fire: fiery sparks, a fireball off the screen, a spark sets the hat alight, it burns unnoticed, a fright, water puts it out.

Rained on: droplets, a stream of water off the screen, a quiet moment, rain, soaked and cross, dries off.

Pranks

Rain on sleeping friends (half the time there are any, on one or more of them at random): the one in the middle laughs, the soaked ones wake up, glare at it, dry off and go back to sleep.

The same, but only one of them gets soaked.

Hat swap (half the time the gust finds friends asleep): the gust takes the hats of two sleeping friends and swaps them; they wake up, toss them back and go back to sleep.

Hat swap with only one friend asleep: it swaps hats with the prankster.

Fireball in the campfire (half the time friends sleep by the fire): it flares up, they're startled, the prankster is ashamed.
